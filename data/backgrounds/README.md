# Background images

The images in this folder are bundled with Flameshot as ready-made
backgrounds for the background editor. They are photos from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), downscaled to 2048 px on the longer side.

They are distributed under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license),
not under the GPL that covers the rest of Flameshot: they can be used for free,
for commercial and non-commercial purposes, but they can't be sold without
significant modification or compiled to replicate a similar or competing
